# Design Pack Guide

A proposed design pack defines a versioned ID, semantic color tokens, typography, spacing, radius, surfaces, borders, motion profile, component variants and layout guidance. It never invents brand assets. Use `../schemas/design-pack.schema.json` for the machine-readable contract.

Build semantic tokens such as `text`, `surface`, `accent`, `on-accent`, `border` and `focus`. Derive buttons and cards from these tokens; verify contrast for each state. Provide system fallbacks for fonts and never assume access to paid assets. Each pack needs desktop/mobile examples, keyboard states and a reduced-motion variant.

Selection: choose one primary style by audience and purpose, then a compatible layout and motion profile. The initial six packs are Conversion Clean, Editorial Premium, Industrial Bold, Product Bento, Mission Control and Playful Launch. See the selection guide and catalogs for alternatives.
