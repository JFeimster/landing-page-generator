# Style Selection Guide

Choose a visual style for the audience and task, then combine it with an appropriate layout and component. These are design directions, not implemented theme packages. Detailed machine-readable attributes live in `../catalogs/design-styles.json`.

| Style | Audience and fit | Type, colors, surfaces, imagery | Layout and component | Motion, accessibility and avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conversion Clean | Service buyers and funnels | Humanist sans, neutral plus accent, flat surfaces, real offer images | Conversion Stack + sticky CTA | Subtle; high contrast; avoid generic stock and immersive campaigns |
| Editorial Premium | High-ticket buyers | Serif display, cream and ink, paper surfaces, approved photography | Editorial Scroll + product demo | Subtle; readable body; poor fit for dense dashboards |
| Industrial Bold | Challenger and agency buyers | Condensed sans, monospace, black/white and safety accent, hard grid, product details | Split-Screen + process timeline | Restrained; preserve focus; avoid all-caps walls or anxious care contexts |
| Product Bento | Software evaluators | Precise sans, neutral accent, modular cards, real screens | Product Showcase + product demo | Subtle; maintain reading order; avoid repetitive cards and long narrative |
| Mission Control | Operators and analysts | Sans and tabular numerals, slate, structured panels and textual data | Dashboard Shell + command palette | Minimal; no color-only status; avoid tiny text and story-led campaigns |
| Playful Launch | Consumer and creator audiences | Rounded display, bright blocks, tactile surfaces, licensed illustrations | Narrative Funnel + animated hero | Optional expressive motion; clear labels; avoid decoration in regulated enterprise flows |
| Swiss Modernist | Professional audiences | Grotesk type, black/white/red, strict grid, editorial image | Editorial Scroll + timeline | Subtle; readable hierarchy; avoid rigid small type for children |
| Neo-Noir | Technical audiences | Geometric type, dark matte surfaces, restrained bright accent, cinematic assets | Product Showcase + demo | Subtle; check dark contrast; avoid neon glare in text-heavy content |
| Retro-Futurist | Creative audiences | Display sans, chrome and ink, geometric layers, labeled concept art | Immersive Canvas + animated hero | Reduced-motion static fallback; avoid scanline illegibility in data tools |
| Warm Founder-Led | Creators and services | Editorial serif, sand and brown, paper, approved founder photos | Editorial Scroll + timeline | Subtle; clear body text; avoid invented founder stories |
| Organic Tactile | Wellness and food | Humanist type, earth and cream, light texture, licensed imagery | Editorial Scroll + comparison | Subtle; texture cannot impair contrast; avoid health promises |
| Kinetic Typography | Campaigns and events | Display typography, limited high-contrast palette, open canvas | Immersive Canvas + animated hero | Static text is essential; avoid motion-dependent reading |
| Glass and Light | Productivity and finance | Clear sans, neutral translucent panels, real UI imagery | Product Showcase + demo | Subtle; solid background fallback; avoid low-contrast blur |

Layouts: Conversion Stack sequences promise, proof and offer; Editorial Scroll alternates narrative and imagery; Product Showcase leads with interface evidence; Narrative Funnel progressively requests inputs; Directory Grid combines search, filters and cards; Dashboard Shell organizes navigation and data; Split-Screen pairs two content regions; Immersive Canvas uses full-viewport scenes with a readable linear fallback.

The catalogs link each style to a recommended layout, component and motion profile. Actual brand constraints and accessibility tests take priority over a catalog suggestion.
