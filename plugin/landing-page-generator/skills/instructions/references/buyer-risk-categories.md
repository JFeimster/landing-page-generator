# Buyer Risk Categories

## Purpose
Use this taxonomy to match the page to the fears, questions, and friction points that matter most.

## Financial Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries the purchase will waste money.
### Signals
- "Is it worth it?"
- "What does it cost?"
### Sections To Address
- Offer stack
- Pricing
- FAQ
- Guarantee
### Copy Strategies
- Clarify included value
- Explain scope
- Reduce ambiguity
### FAQ Angles
- What is included?
- What if it does not fit my needs?
### Proof Needed
- Testimonials
- Case studies
### CTA Friction
Medium to high
### Example Language
"You will know exactly what is included before you commit."

## Time Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries the process will take too long.
### Signals
- "How long does it take?"
- "We need this live fast."
### Sections
- How it works
- Timeline FAQ
- CTA
### Strategies
- Show steps
- Show turnaround
### Proof
- Delivery timeline proof
### Example
"Share the inputs and get a structured draft quickly."

## Trust Risk
### Definition
The buyer is unsure the offer or vendor is credible.
### Signals
- "Can you show examples?"
- "Who are you?"
### Sections
- Founder story
- Trust strip
- Testimonials
### Strategies
- Use proof and transparent process
### Proof
- Real logos
- Real testimonials
### Example
"Here is what we do, who it is for, and what happens next."

## Implementation Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries the solution will be hard to use or deploy.
### Signals
- "Do I need a developer?"
- "What do I need to provide?"
### Sections
- How it works
- FAQ
- HTML notes
### Strategies
- Explain handoff
- Minimize required inputs
### Example
"You can launch with the provided placeholders and replace them later."

## Complexity Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries the offer feels too complicated.
### Signals
- "This seems like a lot"
### Sections
- Hero
- How it works
- FAQ
### Strategies
- Simplify the path
- Reduce jargon

## Social / Status Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries about how the choice reflects on them.
### Signals
- "Will this look professional?"
- "Will my team approve this?"
### Sections
- Design system
- Founder story
- Proof
### Strategies
- Emphasize polish and alignment

## Opportunity Cost Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries that delaying will cost momentum or leads.
### Signals
- "We need to launch soon"
### Sections
- Agitation
- Urgency
### Strategies
- Show the cost of waiting without exaggeration

## Switching Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries moving from an existing solution will be painful.
### Signals
- "We already have a page"
### Sections
- How it works
- FAQ
### Strategies
- Explain migration and compatibility

## Compliance Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries about policy, legal, or regulated requirements.
### Signals
- Finance, health, legal, or sensitive claims
### Sections
- Disclaimer
- FAQ
- Proof rules
### Strategies
- Keep claims conservative

## Performance Risk
### Definition
The buyer worries the page will not perform.
### Signals
- "Will this convert?"
### Sections
- Proof
- Mechanism
- Quality checklist
### Strategies
- Focus on known conversion principles
- Avoid guarantees
