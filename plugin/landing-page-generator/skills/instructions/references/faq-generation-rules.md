# FAQ Generation Rules

## Purpose
Use FAQ to surface and resolve the questions that most often stop a buyer from taking action.

## How To Find Questions
- Pull from user-supplied objections
- Infer likely objections from the offer type
- Use questions that address process, fit, timing, pricing, and risk

## How To Structure Answers
- Answer directly in the first sentence
- Add one supporting sentence
- Avoid long essays
- Use plain language

## Length Guidelines
- Most answers: 2 to 4 sentences
- Complex offers: short paragraph plus bullet list
- Do not overexplain simple points

## Tone Rules
- Calm
- Clear
- Helpful
- No defensiveness

## Trust-Building Rules
- Be specific about process and scope
- State what happens next
- State what the buyer must provide

## Risk Reversal Placement
- Include at least one FAQ that addresses risk, refund, or revision policy when relevant

## Pricing FAQs
- "How much does it cost?"
- "Are there payment plans?"
- "What is included?"

## Implementation FAQs
- "What do you need from me?"
- "How long does setup take?"
- "Can you work from a rough draft?"

## Timeline FAQs
- "How fast can I launch?"
- "When do I get the first draft?"
- "How long does delivery take?"

## Fit / Not-Fit FAQs
- "Who is this for?"
- "Who is this not for?"
- "Do I need an established brand?"

## Technical FAQs
- "Will this work on mobile?"
- "Can it integrate with my form tool?"
- "Do I need a developer?"

## Guarantee FAQs
- "Is there a guarantee?"
- "What if I need revisions?"
- "What if the result is not what I expected?"

## Ethical Claim Rules
- Do not use FAQs to smuggle in unsupported claims
- Do not imply outcomes without proof
- Do not create fake certainty

## FAQ Schema Suggestion
Use FAQPage schema only when the FAQs are actually present on the page and the answers are faithful to the published content.

## Example FAQ Sets By Industry
### SaaS
- How does onboarding work?
- What integrations are supported?
- Is there a trial?

### Coaching
- Who is this for?
- How much time does it take?
- Do I get support?

### Agency
- What do you need from me?
- How many revisions are included?
- How long does it take?

### Local Service
- What areas do you serve?
- How quickly can you arrive?
- Do you offer estimates?

### Ecommerce
- What is the return policy?
- How long does shipping take?
- How do I choose the right size?
