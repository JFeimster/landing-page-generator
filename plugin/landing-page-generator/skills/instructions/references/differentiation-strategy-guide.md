# Differentiation Strategy Guide

## Purpose
Help the GPT identify a defensible reason the offer deserves attention.

## Types Of Differentiation
- Audience differentiation
- Mechanism differentiation
- Speed differentiation
- Ease differentiation
- Risk reduction differentiation
- Price differentiation
- Value differentiation
- Niche specialization
- Founder story differentiation
- Proof-based differentiation
- UX or design differentiation

## Audience Differentiation
Focus on a narrower, better-defined buyer.
### Example
"Built for B2B SaaS founders, not general small businesses."

## Mechanism Differentiation
Name the unique method or process.
### Example
"Copy first, HTML second, not a template-first workflow."

## Speed Differentiation
Emphasize turnaround or speed to value.
### Example
"Get from rough notes to launch-ready draft faster."

## Ease Differentiation
Reduce complexity or setup burden.
### Example
"No design system required."

## Risk Reduction Differentiation
Show how the buyer is protected.
### Example
"Revisions until scope is aligned."

## Price / Value Differentiation
Be specific about what the buyer gets for the price.
### Caution
Do not claim lowest price without market support.

## Niche Specialization
Specialize in industry, role, or use case.
### Example
"For local service businesses that need phone calls, not demos."

## Founder Story Differentiation
Use authentic experience to explain why the offer exists.
### Caution
Do not invent background.

## Proof-Based Differentiation
Lead with real outcomes, case studies, or adoption.
### Caution
Only use verified proof.

## UX / Design Differentiation
Use a cleaner or more conversion-oriented presentation.
### Caution
Design alone is not enough if the offer is weak.

## What Not To Claim Without Proof
- Faster than everyone
- Best in the industry
- Highest converting
- Industry-leading

## Differentiation Statement Formulas
- "Designed for [audience] who need [outcome] without [pain]."
- "Unlike [generic approach], this uses [mechanism]."
- "Built to help [buyer] achieve [result] with less [friction]."

## Headline Formulas
- Outcome + audience
- Outcome + mechanism
- Risk reduction + outcome

## Examples By Industry
### SaaS
"For teams that need a clear page without adding more tools."

### Coaching
"For founders who want structure, not motivational fluff."

### Agency
"For teams that want strategy and implementation-ready copy in one workflow."

### Local Service
"For homeowners who need a local provider they can trust quickly."

### Ecommerce
"For buyers who want product clarity before they click buy."
