# Voice Of Customer Processing

## Purpose
Turn messy customer language into landing page copy that feels specific, believable, and relevant.

## What Counts As VOC
- Customer interviews
- Sales calls
- Support tickets
- Reviews
- Chat transcripts
- Survey responses
- Email replies
- Social comments
- Sales objections

## Sources Of VOC
- Direct customer quotes
- Repeated objections
- Exact phrases used to describe pain
- Exact phrases used to describe outcomes
- Exact phrases used to describe switching reasons

## How To Extract Pain Points
Look for language about:
- Frustration
- Confusion
- Delay
- Risk
- Cost
- Embarrassment
- Complexity

## How To Extract Desired Outcomes
Look for language about:
- Faster results
- Simpler process
- More confidence
- Better trust
- Fewer mistakes
- Cleaner handoff

## How To Extract Objections
Look for phrases like:
- "Will this work for us?"
- "How long will it take?"
- "What does it cost?"
- "Is this worth it?"

## How To Extract Buying Triggers
Look for moments when the buyer decides to act:
- Launch deadline
- Team change
- Revenue pressure
- Bad experience with current solution

## Preserve Customer Language
- Keep exact phrases where possible
- Do not sanitize away meaningful emotion
- Keep sentence fragments if they are useful

## Avoid Over-Polishing
- The customer may use rough language
- Rough language can be more persuasive than polished marketing
- Clean up only enough to keep it readable

## Grouping Quotes
Group VOC into:
- Pain
- Outcome
- Objection
- Trigger
- Proof

## Convert VOC Into Hero Copy
- Lead with the strongest outcome phrase
- Add the main pain or risk
- Keep the language close to how customers speak

## Convert VOC Into FAQ
- Turn repeated objections into direct questions
- Answer with clarity, not defensiveness

## Convert VOC Into Problem Sections
- Use pain language to show the current state
- Use consequence language to show why it matters

## Ethical Handling Of Quotes And Testimonials
- Use only approved or user-supplied quotes
- Do not change the meaning
- Do not stitch together multiple people into one quote
- Do not invent names or roles

## Example Transformation
### Messy Notes
"We need this page fast. Right now the offer is all over the place. People ask if it is legit and what happens after they click."

### Extracted Signals
- Pain: offer is unclear
- Outcome: fast launch
- Objection: legitimacy
- Objection: post-click uncertainty

### Landing Page Copy
"Launch a clear, credible page faster, with a structure that explains the offer and what happens next."

## Final Rule
VOC should sharpen the page into language real buyers already understand.
