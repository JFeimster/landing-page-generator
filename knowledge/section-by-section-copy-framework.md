# Section-by-Section Copy Framework

## How To Use This File
Use these section patterns as modular building blocks. Include only the sections that support the offer and the proof available.

## Hero
### Purpose
State the promise, audience, and differentiator immediately.
### When To Include
Always.
### Copy Formula
`[Desired outcome] for [specific audience] using [unique mechanism], without [main pain or risk].`
### Required Inputs
Offer, audience, core benefit, differentiator.
### Output Structure
- Headline
- Subheadline
- CTA
- Optional proof line
### Placeholder Rules
Use `[Insert proof]` if proof is missing.
### Strong Example
"Launch your next landing page in days, not weeks, with a proven structure built for direct-response offers."
### Weak Example
"Welcome to our website."
### Common Mistakes
- Too broad
- No outcome
- No audience
### Optimization Tips
Use specificity, outcome language, and friction reduction.

## Trust Strip / Social Proof
### Purpose
Reduce doubt quickly.
### When To Include
When proof exists or the brand has recognizable assets.
### Copy Formula
`Trusted by [logos or categories]` or `Used by [type of customer]`.
### Required Inputs
Logos, testimonials, certifications, counts, or categories.
### Output Structure
Short line plus logo row or proof snippets.
### Placeholder Rules
Use `[Logo placeholder]` rather than inventing brands.
### Strong Example
"Used by founders, agencies, and solo operators building conversion-focused pages."
### Weak Example
"Trusted by everyone."
### Common Mistakes
- Fabricated logos
- Too many badges
### Optimization Tips
Prefer fewer, stronger proof signals.

## Problem
### Purpose
Show the cost of the current situation.
### When To Include
Always.
### Copy Formula
Describe the current pain, friction, or missed opportunity.
### Required Inputs
Audience frustrations and desired outcomes.
### Output Structure
Problem statement, symptom bullets, emotional consequence.
### Placeholder Rules
Use customer language where possible.
### Strong Example
"Your page gets traffic, but visitors bounce because the offer is unclear and the CTA is weak."
### Weak Example
"Marketing is hard."
### Common Mistakes
- Over-dramatizing
- Generic pain points
### Optimization Tips
Anchor pain to a concrete business cost.

## Agitation
### Purpose
Amplify the cost of inaction without manipulation.
### When To Include
When the audience needs urgency.
### Copy Formula
Show what gets worse if nothing changes.
### Required Inputs
Time, money, or opportunity costs.
### Output Structure
Short paragraph or bullets.
### Placeholder Rules
Do not invent losses.
### Strong Example
"Every extra revision cycle delays launch, delays learning, and delays revenue."
### Weak Example
"You will fail."
### Common Mistakes
- Fearmongering
### Optimization Tips
Keep it sharp and factual.

## Solution Mechanism
### Purpose
Explain why this offer works.
### When To Include
Always if the offer has a real method.
### Copy Formula
`This works because [mechanism]`.
### Required Inputs
Process, method, framework, or unique delivery model.
### Output Structure
Short explanation plus supporting bullets.
### Strong Example
"We use a conversion-first structure that moves from promise to proof to CTA."
### Weak Example
"Our secret sauce is amazing."
### Common Mistakes
- Vague buzzwords
### Optimization Tips
Name the mechanism in plain language.

## How It Works
### Purpose
Reduce implementation anxiety.
### When To Include
When the purchase or onboarding is nontrivial.
### Copy Formula
`Step 1, Step 2, Step 3`.
### Required Inputs
Real process steps.
### Output Structure
Numbered steps.
### Strong Example
"1. Share your offer. 2. Review the draft. 3. Launch and test."
### Weak Example
"It's easy."
### Common Mistakes
- Too many steps
### Optimization Tips
Make it feel simple and controlled.

## Features And Benefits
### Purpose
Translate capabilities into outcomes.
### When To Include
Always if the offer has concrete deliverables.
### Copy Formula
`Feature -> benefit -> why it matters`.
### Required Inputs
Offer details.
### Output Structure
Bullets or cards.
### Strong Example
"Mobile-first layout - helps more visitors read and act on phones."
### Weak Example
"Responsive design."
### Common Mistakes
- Listing features without payoff
### Optimization Tips
Lead with outcomes.

## Founder Story
### Purpose
Increase trust through origin and context.
### When To Include
When the founder story strengthens credibility.
### Copy Formula
`Why this exists + what was learned + why it matters now`.
### Strong Example
"Built after seeing too many pages with strong products and weak messaging."

## Case Studies
### Purpose
Demonstrate results with specifics.
### When To Include
Only with user-supplied proof.
### Placeholder Rules
If absent, use `[Case study placeholder]`.
### Strong Example
"[Client] improved [metric] after [change]."

## Testimonials
### Purpose
Reduce skepticism with third-party voice.
### When To Include
Only when supplied or publicly verifiable.
### Placeholder Rules
Use "[Testimonial placeholder]" if missing.
### Strong Example
"[Customer quote about clarity, speed, or results]."

## Offer Stack
### Purpose
Show everything included and why it is valuable.
### When To Include
Always for paid offers.
### Copy Formula
Core offer + deliverables + bonuses + support + guarantee.

## Bonuses
### Purpose
Increase perceived value or reduce friction.
### When To Include
When bonuses are real and helpful.
### Strong Example
"Bonus: launch checklist and CTA swipe file."

## Guarantee
### Purpose
Reduce risk.
### When To Include
Only if true and approved.
### Strong Example
"If the deliverable does not match the agreed scope, we will revise it."

## Urgency
### Purpose
Encourage timely action.
### When To Include
Only when there is a real deadline or capacity limit.
### Placeholder Rules
Use `[Deadline]`.

## Scarcity
### Purpose
Explain real constraints.
### When To Include
Only when genuine.
### Strong Example
"We take 5 builds per month to keep turnaround fast."

## FAQ
### Purpose
Handle objections.
### When To Include
Always near the bottom.
### Strong Example
"How fast can I launch? We will use placeholders until your final assets are ready."

## Final CTA
### Purpose
Convert when interest is highest.
### When To Include
Always.
### Copy Formula
`Start [desired action]` or `Get [offer]`.
### Strong Example
"Get your landing page draft."
