# Pricing Psychology Guide

## Core Rule
Pricing must come from the user or be shown as a placeholder. Do not invent pricing, discounts, or savings.

## Anchoring
- Show the main price in a clear context.
- Use comparison only when factual.
- Do not create fake high anchors.

## Value Stacking
- Frame the price against the included deliverables.
- Explain what is included before discussing cost.

## Cost Of Inaction
- Show what the buyer loses by waiting.
- Keep it factual and relevant.

## Payment Plans
- Use when the real offer supports them.
- Explain whether the total price changes.
- Do not imply affordability that does not exist.

## Price Framing
- Frame around outcome and scope.
- Use "starting at" only if accurate.
- Avoid vague "contact us for pricing" unless the offer truly requires it.

## Decoy Pricing
- Only if the user intentionally has multiple tiers.
- Do not invent a decoy tier.

## Good / Better / Best
- Best for tiered offers.
- Keep each tier meaningfully different.
- Do not overload with trivial changes.

## Subscription Vs. One-Time Framing
- Subscription: emphasize ongoing access, updates, or support.
- One-time: emphasize ownership, speed, and clarity.
- Match framing to the actual billing model.

## Discount Ethics
- Never fake a sale.
- Never claim a discount from an invented prior price.
- If discounted, state the real terms plainly.

## Bonus Valuation Rules
- Only value bonuses with real market relevance.
- Avoid inflated dollar values unless supported.

## ROI Framing
- Can be used when the buyer can reasonably expect a return.
- Use carefully in regulated or uncertain categories.
- Never present ROI as guaranteed.

## Avoid Fake Savings
- Do not calculate savings from numbers the user did not provide.
- Do not invent retail values.

## Financial Claim Caution
- Avoid "this will pay for itself" unless supported by the user.
- Avoid specific earnings promises unless verified and permitted.

## Example Pricing Sections With Placeholders
### Simple
"Pricing: [Enter price]. Includes [deliverables]."

### Tiered
"Starter: [price]. Pro: [price]. Enterprise: [price]."

### Subscription
"Plans start at [price]/month and include [support or access]."

## Best Practices
- Put price near the action.
- Explain what happens after purchase.
- Address price objections in the FAQ.
- Keep pricing language consistent with the actual checkout flow.
