# Landing Page Quality Checklist

## Scoring Rubric
Rate each item from 1 to 5.
- 1 = missing or weak
- 3 = acceptable
- 5 = strong and ready to ship

## Checklist
- Clear offer
- Clear audience
- Strong hero
- Specific pain points
- Strong solution mechanism
- Benefits are clearer than features
- Proof is real and relevant
- No unsupported claims
- CTA is obvious and repeated
- Offer stack is easy to understand
- Pricing is accurate or clearly placeholdered
- Guarantee or risk reversal is truthful
- Urgency and scarcity are ethical
- FAQ answers likely objections
- Mobile layout reads well
- Accessibility basics are present
- SEO metadata is included
- HTML is self-contained and implementable
- Placeholders are explicit and complete
- Analytics placeholders exist if needed
- Performance is lightweight
- Final output includes all requested sections

## Pass Criteria
- 4 or 5 on the hero, proof, CTA, and offer clarity
- No score below 3 on proof integrity or unsupported claims
- No missing critical placeholders

## Failure Signals
- The page sounds generic
- The offer is unclear after reading the hero
- Proof is fabricated or overclaimed
- CTA labels do not match buyer intent
- Mobile copy becomes too dense
- FAQs avoid the hard questions

## Section-Level Checks
### Hero
- Outcome stated
- Audience stated
- Differentiator stated

### Proof
- Proof is real
- Proof is labeled
- Proof matches the promise

### Offer
- Includes what is being sold
- Includes what is not included if needed
- Includes next step

### CTA
- Clear action
- Low-friction wording
- Visible at top and bottom

### HTML
- Semantic structure
- Accessible controls
- Responsive layout
- Metadata present

## Final QA Gate
Before publishing, confirm:
1. No fake testimonials or logos
2. No unsupported guarantees
3. No invented pricing
4. No broken placeholders
5. No missing CTA path
6. No text that requires hidden context to understand
