# Competitor Analysis Framework

## Purpose
Extract public positioning signals from competitor landing pages so the GPT can identify differentiation opportunities.

## Public-Page-Only Rule
- Use only publicly visible content.
- Do not scrape private, login-gated, or paywalled material.
- Do not infer hidden pricing or hidden terms unless clearly shown.

## What To Extract
- Positioning
- Ideal customer
- Main promise
- Offer structure
- CTA language
- Proof style
- Pricing
- Guarantee
- Page sections
- Design patterns
- Messaging gaps

## Positioning
- What problem do they claim to solve?
- For whom?
- With what mechanism?

## ICP
- Who appears to be the primary buyer?
- What language suggests seniority, industry, or use case?

## Promise
- What outcome is promised?
- Is it specific or vague?

## Offer
- What is included?
- What is excluded?
- Is the offer productized or consultative?

## CTAs
- What action do they want?
- Is the CTA high-friction or low-friction?

## Proof
- Testimonials
- Logos
- Metrics
- Badges
- Case studies

## Pricing
- Visible price
- Tier structure
- "Contact sales" patterns

## Guarantees
- Explicit guarantees
- Refund language
- Risk reversal language

## Page Sections
- Hero
- Proof strip
- Benefits
- Process
- FAQ
- Footer CTA

## Design Patterns
- Dense or minimal
- Bold or restrained
- Illustrated or screenshot-led

## Messaging Gaps
- Missing proof
- Generic promise
- Weak CTA
- Unclear niche

## Differentiation Opportunities
- Narrower audience
- Better mechanism
- Stronger proof
- Easier onboarding
- Better risk reversal

## Ethical Rules
- Do not copy a competitor's wording too closely
- Do not exaggerate weaknesses that are not factual
- Do not imply you have verified hidden data
